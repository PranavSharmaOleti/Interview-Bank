### From GME Entrance Exam Syllabus (standard mechanical engineering)
- Engineering Mechanics: free-body diagrams, equilibrium, friction, trusses, virtual work, kinematics/dynamics
- Mechanics of Materials: stress/strain, elastic constants, Mohr's circle, thin cylinders, shear stress, beam deflection, torsion, Euler columns
- Theory of Machines: displacement/velocity/acceleration analysis, cams, gears, flywheels, governors, balancing
- Vibrations: free/forced SDOF, damping, isolation, resonance, critical speeds
- Machine Design: static/dynamic loading, failure theories, fatigue S-N diagram, bolted/riveted/welded joints, shafts, gears, bearings, brakes, clutches, springs
- Fluid Mechanics: fluid properties, fluid statics, submerged bodies, control-volume analysis, Bernoulli, dimensional analysis, viscous flow, boundary layer, pipe flow, head losses, compressible flow
- Heat-Transfer: modes, 1D conduction, fins, unsteady conduction, convective heat transfer, heat exchangers (LMTD/NTU), radiation
- Thermodynamics: systems/properties, pure substances, ideal/real gases, 1st/2nd laws, property charts, availability/irreversibility
- Materials/Manufacturing/Industrial: structure/properties, phase diagrams, heat treatment, casting/forging/forming, machining, metrology, CIM, production planning
